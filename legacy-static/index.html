OUR 5-STEP PROCESS

Your solar journey,
made simple.

From the first conversation to long-term support, we’re with you at every step.

  1. Site visit

    We listen to your energy needs and assess your site, roof and installation requirements.

  2. Customised proposal

    We design a system around your usage, available space and budget.

  3. Paperwork & assistance

    We guide you through documents, subsidy steps and loan assistance if needed.

  4. Professional installation

    Our team handles the electrical and civil work, plus site-specific grid connection.

  5. Continued support

    We stay available for monitoring, maintenance and help after installation.
