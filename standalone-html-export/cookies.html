SITE TECHNOLOGY

Cookie Policy

Cookies are small files a website or its service providers may place in your browser. Here is what we currently see on Sunergy’s public site.

Last updated: 18 September 2026

Cookies currently used

CookieProviderPurpose
__Host-appgarden-visitorWebsite hosting platformPlatform visitor/operation cookie. Its exact duration is controlled by the host.
cf_clearanceCloudflareRecords that a visitor passed a security challenge so it need not be repeated immediately.
__cf_bmCloudflareHelps distinguish automated traffic and protect the site.

Cookie presence and duration can vary with browser settings and security checks. The current site code does not set analytics or advertising cookies and does not use browser local storage for your calculator inputs.

External services

Google Fonts loads font files from Google servers, which receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address. Choosing the WhatsApp enquiry button opens WhatsApp; WhatsApp may then use its own cookies or app storage under its policies. Our Privacy Policy explains these data flows.

Your controls

You can block or clear cookies in your browser settings. Blocking security or platform cookies may interrupt access to the site. Because the website currently has no optional analytics or advertising cookies in its code, there is no optional-cookie switch on this site. If we add such tools, we will update this policy and provide any choices required before using them.
